Service in five steps

From the first call to the money in

Heavenly Greens · Service department · updated October 30, 2026

Every service job is one ticket, on one calendar, worked from one board: HG Command → Service. As of October 30, service has left Salesforce: every visit is booked in AGM or HG Command, and nowhere else.

The five steps

Step Who
1 Book it, and choose: photo link or not Call center
2 It shows up on the Service board Automatic
3 Price it and schedule it DeAnna, or hand it to the call center
4 The crew gets its work order link DeAnna → crew
5 Close it and collect Crew, then automatic

Everyone in the screenshots that follow is a made-up customer.

Step 1 · The call center books it

On the customer's AGM contact, press Service Request. Type the caller's phone and press Look up: it says whether they've called before and whether HG installed at that address. Not in our system? Choose New customer — not in our system at the bottom. Type what's wrong in their words, add any gate / access notes (they print on the crew's sheet), then press Create the service ticket.

Step 1 · Photo link, or no link?

  • Send the link: it now goes by email and text unless the customer asks for one; if we only have one, it uses that. Type your name and press the send button. It is never sent automatically.
  • No link, it needs a look: book an assessment visit, or mark it Needs an in-person look so the call center calls to book. Nothing goes to the customer.
  • No link, it's a clear job: book it on the Service calendar. Anyone on staff can book; a crew that is already busy at that time is refused.

Once the link goes out: what the customer gets

The link opens their own ticket page. It shows which four photos to take, takes photos and short videos, and promises an answer within 24 to 72 hours. No photos yet? The system reminds them twice, 72 hours apart, the way they asked, then stops. When they press Send my photos, the ticket moves to Needs a price by itself and service@ gets an email.

Step 2 · It shows up on the Service board

Within minutes the ticket is on hgcommand.com → Service, assigned to DeAnna. The board now reads in plain words: Customers waiting on us · Your to-do list · Open tickets · Finished · Today's numbers. Each card leads with the customer's name and has one green button. Every ticket also has ✓ Close ticket and More ▾, which only offers what fits the ticket's step.

Step 2 · Waiting on photos, and the chase

  • Link just sent: a grey line saying who sent it and when. Nothing to do yet; the reminders go out by themselves.
  • Nothing back after 72 hours: it becomes Chase. Press Resend the link, call and offer an assessment visit, or use More ▾ → Needs an in-person look.
  • Photos arrive: the card becomes Price it, the next step.

Step 3 · DeAnna prices it

Enter the final price, or choose no charge with the reason (warranty, go-back, free tune-up, goodwill). No-charge work gets a crew but never an invoice. Then assign a crew and press Save changes to this ticket.

Step 3 · Schedule it, or hand it to the call center

  • Book it yourself: Book the visit on the ticket, or + Book here on the crew's row of the Service calendar. The ticket turns Scheduled and the customer gets one confirmation.
  • Hand it to the call center: More ▾ → Send to the call center to book, and pick how long: 1, 2, 4 or 6 hours, or a full day. It lands on the board's For the call center list, and tops the + Book here picker as "📞 ready to book, 4 hours".
  • Plans change: More ▾ → Unbook cancels the visit on AGM's calendar and puts the ticket back where it was.

Step 4 · The crew's work order link

Crews never get email. Every afternoon DeAnna presses Copy tomorrow for each crew and pastes the link into that crew's group chat. On their phone the crew sees every stop: the address with Open in Maps, the gate / access notes, what the customer said, their photos, and the agreed price or No charge.

Step 5 · Close it and collect

At each stop the crew presses one result. Done: take payment sends the invoice and shows Take payment now, and the customer pays by card on the spot. For a check or Paymentech, use the board's 💳 Collect payment, which opens Record a payment on the ticket. Once paid, by itself: the sales rep is told, and accounting@ and service@ both get "Service paid". Work done with nothing to collect? ✓ Close ticket → Service completed (no message goes to the customer).

Who presses what

When Who Press
A customer calls Call center Service Request on their AGM contact
They can show us Call center or DeAnna Send the self-assessment link (email and text)
No photos after 72 hours DeAnna Resend the link, or More ▾ → Needs an in-person look
Photos are in DeAnna Set a price (or no charge), assign a crew
Ready to book DeAnna, or the call center Book the visit / + Book here, or More ▾ → Send to the call center to book
Visit has to move Anyone booking More ▾ → Unbook, then book again
Afternoon before DeAnna Copy tomorrow into each crew's group chat
At the stop Crew One result: Done: take payment, paid by check, no charge…
A check arrives DeAnna or accounting 💳 Collect payment → Record a payment
Work finished DeAnna ✓ Close ticket → Service completed
