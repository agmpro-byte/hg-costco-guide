The rule: every text, email and call with a customer happens in the AGM app. A reply from Outlook never reaches AGM, so the office, the call center and your follow-up reminders can't see it.

Chapter 1: Get the app and sign in

Same steps on both phones, apart from the store.

IPHONEInstall Artificial Grass Marketing from the App Store

Search artificial grass marketing and tap Get on the one with the green AGM icon. The store name has not changed to AGM Pro Tools yet.

ANDROIDInstall Artificial Grass Marketing from Google Play

Search artificial grass marketing and tap Install on the one with the green AGM icon.

IPHONE & ANDROIDSign in

Use the same email and password you use for AGM on your laptop, then tap Sign In.

IPHONE & ANDROIDPick where your security code goes

Choose Email or Phone and tap Continue.

IPHONE & ANDROIDType the code

Type the 6-digit code and tap Verify and Sign In. You land in Heavenly Greens.

Chapter 2: iPhone settings

Three switches that decide whether your phone tells you a customer wrote back.

IPHONEAllow notifications

Tap Allow. Without it, replies and calls reach the app but your phone never alerts you.

IPHONEAllow the microphone

The first time you call from the app, tap OK, or the customer can't hear you.

IPHONECheck it in Settings

Missed a pop-up? iPhone Settings → Artificial Grass Marketing → turn on Microphone, Notifications and Background App Refresh.

Chapter 3: Android settings

Android also puts apps to sleep to save battery. Turn that off for this one.

ANDROIDAllow notifications

Tap Allow. Without it, replies and calls reach the app but your phone never alerts you.

ANDROIDAllow the microphone

Tap While using the app, so customers can hear you on calls.

ANDROIDSet the battery to Unrestricted

Phone Settings → Apps → Artificial Grass Marketing → Battery → Unrestricted (Samsung: App battery usage). Otherwise calls stop ringing.

Chapter 4: Answer customers in the app

Same on both phones.

THE RULENever press Reply in Outlook

If a customer's email lands in Outlook, don't answer it there. Open the AGM app instead.

IPHONE & ANDROIDOpen Conversations and tap the customer

Every text, email and call with that customer is on one page, the same page the office sees.

IPHONE & ANDROIDSwitch the reply to Email, then Send

The reply box starts on text. Tap the channel button above it and choose Email. It goes out from your Heavenly Greens email and stays on the customer's record.

IPHONE & ANDROIDCall from the app too

Tap the phone icon at the top of the conversation. The call is logged on the customer's record.

Chapter 5: Test it

Three checks before you leave.

  1. Open Conversations. You should see your own customers. If you see nobody's, you may be signed in to the wrong account.
  2. Lock your phone. The next time a customer replies, your phone should buzz with an alert from the app. If it never buzzes, go back to the notification steps.
  3. Send your next customer email from the app, then check it shows up in that customer's conversation.
A step looks different on your phone, or the app never buzzes? Tell Andrew and he'll set it up with you.
